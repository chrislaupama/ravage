## Build brief

Start by blocking out the Crucible atrium with plain brushes. Test the bridge sightline, ramp width, overhead clearance and routes around the centre cover with actual movement before adding detail. Relay is a complementary space study, not a validated connected map layout.

Use charcoal structure, midtone combat surfaces, ivory service inserts and restrained red/cyan landmarks. Keep weapon and pickup identity colours from Bone Circuit distinct from environmental decoration. Put small mechanical details in recesses, outside movement paths.

These sheets communicate shape, materials and mood. Their perspective and axonometric views are illustrative and may disagree. Reconcile them into one blockout; determine dimensions using the game's player bounds and movement. Do not copy generated railings, cover heights or stairs without checking collision, jumping and visibility. Lighting, reflections and material detail are artistic targets, not evidence of current engine rendering capabilities.

Build a small reusable kit first: wall bay, corner pier, doorway, floor tile and inset light. Apply detail only after the room works in play. Validate enemy readability using current player skins and test frame time in the engine.
